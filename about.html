About Gyankunja

Gyankunja is an academic note-sharing platform designed to make BIT learning resources easier to discover, organize and access semester by semester.

Our Purpose

A single academic hub for the BIT community

Students often receive notes, lab files, question banks, notices and schedules through many disconnected channels. Gyankunja brings those materials together in one clear platform so students can spend less time searching and more time learning.

The public resource section lets anyone browse notes from Semester I to Semester VIII, while a signed-in student receives a dashboard focused on their current semester.
